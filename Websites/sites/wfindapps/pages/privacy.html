Privacy

The short version: your documents never leave your computer, and these websites don't track you.

{{privacy_panel}}

The programs

WCopyfind and WRepeatfind are ordinary Windows programs that run entirely on your own PC.

  • Your documents stay where they are. The programs open your files, read their text into memory, compare it, and write a report. They do not upload, transmit, or share your documents or their contents with anyone.
  • Reports are saved on your computer. Reports are web pages saved in a folder you choose (by default, a folder in your Documents). Your web browser opens them from your own disk; they are not published online.
  • No account, no sign-in, no telemetry. The programs don't collect usage statistics, check for updates, or contact any server.
  • Settings stay local. Your settings and document lists are saved in your Windows user profile so the programs remember them next time.
  • The one exception is your choice. WCopyfind can compare a web page if you give it an internet shortcut (a .url file). In that case it downloads that page, just as a web browser would, in order to read it. It never sends your documents anywhere. WRepeatfind doesn't use the internet at all.

You don't have to take our word for it: both programs are open source, and anyone can read exactly what they do in the source code on GitHub.

These websites

  • WFindApps.org, WCopyfind.WFindApps.org, and WRepeatfind.WFindApps.org are plain informational websites. They use no cookies, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking scripts, and they have no forms that collect personal information.
  • Like every web server, ours keeps standard access logs (such as the address that requested a page, the time, and the browser type) for security and to keep the sites running. These logs aren't sold or shared, and they're not combined with any other information about you.
  • The program downloads are served by GitHub, which has its own privacy statement.
