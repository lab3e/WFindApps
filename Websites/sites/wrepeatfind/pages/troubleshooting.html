Troubleshooting

Windows says "Windows protected your PC"

Click More info, then Run anyway. SmartScreen shows this for programs that aren't signed with a paid certificate.

WRepeatfind finds nothing, but I know something is repeated
  • The repeat may be shorter than Report phrases of at least. Try 4 or 5 words.
  • The copies may differ by more words in a row than you allow. Raise Allow up to ___ differing words in a row, or lower the matching percentage in More Options.
  • If the repeat is between two files, make sure Check the documents together as one work is ticked.
  • A scanned PDF contains no text to read; check the original document instead.
It finds too many small repeats

Common phrases ("on the other hand", "at the same time") appear everywhere. Raise Report phrases of at least to 8 or 10 words to see only substantial repeats, and dismiss the rest.

A file can't be read

It may be open in another program that locks it, damaged, password-protected, or not really the type its extension says. Close it elsewhere, or save a fresh copy as .docx or .txt. You can also copy its text and use Paste Text.

Strange characters appear in the report

A plain-text file without a UTF-8 marker is read with Windows' standard character set. In Notepad, choose Save as, set Encoding to UTF-8, and check again. Or check the original Word document.

The report doesn't open

Click Open Report, or open the report file in Documents\WRepeatfind Reports yourself. The report opens automatically only when repeats are found, and only if that option is on in More Options.

My dismissed repeats came back

Dismissals are remembered by your web browser, for that document, by the passage's words. They come back if you edited the passage, opened the report in a different browser, cleared your browser's site data, or renamed the document.

The paragraph numbers don't match my word processor

WRepeatfind counts a paragraph each time the text starts a new line, and doesn't count empty lines. Headings, list items, and table cells each count as paragraphs. In plain-text files with a line break at the end of every line, each line counts.

Still stuck? Please report the problem and include the version number from the title bar.
